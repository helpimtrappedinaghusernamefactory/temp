Game opens. Several protagonists, in order from first to last deaths. After each dies, return to character select screen with their portrait missing. Can be chosen out of order, if you’re weird.


Jessa - Running home in suburbia, annihilated by a nuke. 
Ezekiel - Horribly scarred, begs someone to give water, but keels over and dies.
Tobias - Riding a motorcycle, mad-max style. It flips and he instantly dies.
Rudolph - Running from Tobias’ gang, the Bluebells, before being shot.
Diane - Member of the Bluebells, gunned down by the mounted gun Sadie and Ashley have in front of their shack.
Sacalil - Caught in the crossfire of Sadie/Ashley vs Bluebells.
Sadie - Laying in bed with Ashley at her side, telling her that it’s the end. Ashley doesn't wanna believe it, Sadie passes quietly.

Sacalil’s portrait reappears, screen turned off with a bullet hole through it. Game actually starts through this option.

Wake up on a toolbench in a cave. Stumble out of the cave, just below Ashley’s shack, with Sadie’s grave next to it. She’s painting. Ashley recognizes you as the robot Sadie was trying to save, and gives you back your bandana. She’s grumpy and kicks you out. Once you leave, she throws her paint can through the roof, yelling GOD DAMMIT! She’s out of blue paint, and needs more, complaining about how hard paint is to find nowadays. You can go beat up Myo, and hold the paint hostage for Ashley to join your party.
